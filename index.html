About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Duke University and an affiliated researcher at the Nicholas Institute for Energy, Environment & Sustainability.

During 2024–2025, I served as a Staff Economist at the White House Council of Economic Advisers, where my portfolio spanned climate policy, energy, and industrial strategy. Prior to starting my Ph.D., I worked as an Associate Economist at the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago and as a Research Assistant at the University of Chicago Urban Labs.

I hold an M.A. in public policy from the University of Chicago and bachelor's degrees from the University of Georgia.
